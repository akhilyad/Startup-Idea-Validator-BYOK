## Brand & Style

This design system draws inspiration from classic literary publishing and thoughtful technological craftsmanship. The emotional response is one of intellectual calm, unhurried clarity, and conversational warmth. Rather than relying on cold digital minimalism or utilitarian enterprise tropes, it treats digital interfaces like fine print: tactile, composed, and humanistic.

The system mixes modern editorial restraint with subtle tactile accents. It prioritizes generous negative space, refined typographic hierarchy, and gentle surface definition over aggressive elevation or saturated visuals. The interface should feel like high-grade unbleached paper holding sharp, considered typography—inviting contemplation, reducing cognitive fatigue, and elevating knowledge work into a calm, focused craft.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in parchment tones and natural mineral pigments, avoiding stark digital black and pure sterile white.

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas (`#FAF8F5`): Primary application background, soft and gentle on the eyes.
  - Surface muted (`#F5F1EB`): Secondary containers, sidebars, and input wells.
  - Surface elevated (`#FFFFFF`): High-priority cards, dropdowns, and modal dialogs.
  - Surface border (`#E8E4DC`): Architectural dividing lines, hairline borders, and subtle structural frames.

- **Typography & Ink:**
  - Primary text (`#1F1E1D`): Deep charcoal, carrying the authority of printed ink without the harsh contrast of `#000000`.
  - Secondary text (`#4A4742`): Warm medium graphite for metadata, supporting body copy, and secondary labels.
  - Muted text (`#76726B`): Tertiary details, disabled states, and subtle hints.

- **Accents:**
  - Primary Accent (`#CC5A36`): Warm terracotta/clay, used purposefully for high-value interactive calls-to-action, key highlights, and active indicators.
  - Accent Hover (`#B84D2B`): Deeper clay for interaction states.
  - Accent Soft (`#F6ECE7`): Tinted background fill for active states, tags, and inline callouts.

## Typography

The typographical pairing forms the core of the brand experience. 

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set in `Newsreader`, an editorial serif with optical proportions that deliver warmth, academic precision, and an immersive cadence. Large headings leverage italics selectively for emphasis, literary atmosphere, and conceptual highlights.
- **Body & UI Elements:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`, providing clean horizontal metrics, generous counterspaces, and modern legibility. It keeps interface tools, navigation, and dense informational tables sharp and effortlessly readable.
- **Rhythm & Line Length:** Body passages must respect optimal readability bounds (45–75 characters per line). Headings prioritize sentence casing to retain an approachable, human voice.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built around a structured fluid grid that privileges generous margins, avoiding visual clutter.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (4-column grid, compact 1rem gutters, 1.25rem edge margin).
  - Tablet: `640px – 1024px` (8-column grid, 1.5rem gutters, 2rem edge margin).
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12-column grid, max content constraint of 1280px, 3rem edge margin).

- **Spacing Philosophy:**
  Vertical rhythm follows generous step intervals. Section blocks breathe with extensive vertical padding (`space-xl` or larger), framing content blocks like plates in a book. Component interiors utilize tight, structured spacing (`space-sm` to `space-md`), creating unified objects that float naturally within the canvas.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies primarily on subtle tonal layering, low-contrast hairline borders, and warm ambient shadows, bypassing flat starkness or heavy synthetic drops.

- **Tonal Tiers:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#FAF8F5` provides the baseline floor.
  - **Level 1 (Recessed/Secondary):** `#F5F1EB` defines distinct working areas, sidebars, or tool trays.
  - **Level 2 (Raised Cards & Panels):** `#FFFFFF` paired with a 1px border of `#E8E4DC` and a delicate, warm ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(44, 40, 37, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(44, 40, 37, 0.03)`.
  - **Level 3 (Floating Menus & Popovers):** `#FFFFFF` paired with `#E8E4DC` border and diffused ambient depth: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(44, 40, 37, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(44, 40, 37, 0.04)`.

- **Border Treatment:** Lines and outlines use warm stone-tinted borders (`#E8E4DC`) at 1px thickness. High-contrast strokes are avoided in favor of quiet structural boundary lines.

## Shapes

The geometric personality balances organic friendliness with editorial discipline:

- **Cards & Surfaces:** Leverage standard roundedness (`0.5rem` / `8px`) up to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) for larger canvas panels. Corners are gentle, never aggressively boxed nor overly bulbous.
- **Buttons, Badges & Inputs:** Interactive trigger components (such as primary buttons, search pills, and status tags) adopt fully rounded pill profiles (`rounded-full`) or smooth rounded corners (`0.75rem`), emphasizing touchability and conversational ease.
- **Inner Containers:** Inner child elements nested inside cards always reflect concentric border radii, keeping the visual nesting proportional.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), warm terracotta fill (`#CC5A36`), white text, subtle hover transition (`#B84D2B`). Padding: `0.625rem 1.25rem`.
  - *Secondary:* Pill-shaped, warm off-white surface (`#FAF8F5`) with subtle border (`#E8E4DC`), charcoal text (`#1F1E1D`), hovering to `#F5F1EB`.
  - *Ghost/Tertiary:* Borderless, neutral text, transitioning to `#F5F1EB` background on hover.

- **Chips & Badges:**
  - Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height: 28px. Light terracotta background tint (`#F6ECE7`) with terracotta label (`#CC5A36`) for active metadata, or light neutral (`#EFECE6`) with dark graphite text (`#4A4742`) for categories and metadata.

- **Cards:**
  - Base background `#FFFFFF` framed by a 1px `#E8E4DC` border, `rounded-xl` (16px radius), and soft ambient shadow. Internal padding is generous (`1.5rem` to `2rem`). Interactive variants apply a subtle upward translation (`-1px`) and gentle border deepening (`#D8D3C8`) on hover.

- **Input Fields:**
  - Background `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F1EB`, border `#E8E4DC`, roundedness `0.75rem` (12px). Placeholder text in warm graphite (`#76726B`). Focus state utilizes a terracotta ring highlight (`rgba(204, 90, 54, 0.2)`) with a crisp border focus (`#CC5A36`).

- **Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios):**
  - Checkboxes use `4px` corner radii; radios are circular. Both feature a 1.5px `#D8D3C8` border on off-state, transitioning to solid `#CC5A36` with crisp white icon checkmarks when selected.

- **Lists & Feed Items:**
  - Dividers are subtle hairlines (`#E8E4DC`). List items provide comfortable hit areas with vertical padding (`1rem`) and gentle hover feedback using `#F5F1EB`.
