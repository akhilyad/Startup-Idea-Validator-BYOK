# Security & Privacy Policy

## 🔒 BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) & Privacy Guarantees

Startup Idea Validator is engineered specifically to protect founder confidentiality and prevent unauthorized API key exposure:

1. **Zero Key Retention & No Centralized Proxy:**
   - Your API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter) are strictly utilized to authenticate client-side requests or your local container environment.
   - We do not run middleman telemetry servers capturing your keys or user payloads.

2. **Proprietary Idea Protection:**
   - Your concept drafts, assumption graphs, and generated validation teardowns remain in your local storage or your self-hosted database instance.
   - We never sell, log, or ingest user prompts into fine-tuning corpora.

3. **Zero Markup, Direct Wholesale:**
   - Calls go directly against the provider APIs at raw cost without vendor commission or token scraping.
