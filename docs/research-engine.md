# Sift — Research Engine & Pipeline

## Pipeline Execution Stages
1. **Stage 1 — Normalization (PRD §9)**: Decomposes unstructured concept into customer, problem, solution, geography, and business model.
2. **Stage 2 — Assumption Extraction (PRD §10)**: Derives decision-critical assumptions across Desirability, Feasibility, Viability, and Defensibility.
3. **Stage 3 — Research Agents (PRD §12)**: Executes modular agents (Problem, Customer, Competitor, Market, Pricing, Technology, Regulation, Economics, GTM, Defensibility).
4. **Stage 4 — Verifying Assumptions (PRD §15, §16)**: Classifies evidence state (`SUPPORTED`, `MIXED`, `CONTRADICTED`, `UNKNOWN`).
5. **Stage 5 — Challenge Mode (PRD §4, §35)**: Specifically searches for disconfirming signals, failed competitors, and complaints.
6. **Stage 6 — Synthesis (PRD §28)**: Generates the falsifiable thesis document.
7. **Stage 7 — Experiment Generation (PRD §26)**: Designs high-yield, low-cost experiments.
8. **Stage 8 — Quality Gate (PRD §59, §60)**: Audits citation coverage and decides `COMPLETE` vs `PARTIAL`.
